toss.

AirDrop for any laptop and any phone.

Copy on your laptop, it is on your phone. Photos, files, links, text, both ways. Pair once and forget about it.

iPhone Android Windows Mac Linux

One minute, once.

  1. Install

    pip install "toss @ git+https://github.com/Swastikkkkk/toss"

  2. Run it

    toss

    It moves to your tray and starts with your laptop from now on. Close the terminal.

  3. Scan the QR with your phone

    It connects by itself. Tap Add to Home Screen and it opens like an app.

Pair once

No sessions, no logging in. Your laptop starts toss on boot and your phone reconnects on its own.

Everything

Photos, PDFs, docs, links, any file. Anything copied while your phone was away is waiting when you open it.

Private

Encrypted end to end, straight over your wifi. No account, no cloud, nothing stored.

Needs both devices on the same wifi. Some college and office networks block that; a phone hotspot works.
