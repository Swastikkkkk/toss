Setup

Install, run, scan. Done.

  1. Install it

    pip install "toss[qr] @ git+https://github.com/Swastikkkkk/toss"

    Python 3.10 or newer. On Windows it lives in your tray.

  2. Start it

    toss

  3. Scan the QR with your phone camera

    It connects by itself. Nothing to type, nothing to install, no warnings. Add to home screen and it reconnects on its own every time.

That is it.

It runs in the background and starts when you log in, so close the terminal. Copy anything and it is on your phone. Paired for good, you never do this again.

toss stop

Turns it off. toss uninstall stops it starting on login.

Encrypted

The four words become an AES-256 key. Device to device over your wifi, no server, nothing stored.

Clipboard sync

Text, images and files, both ways. Password manager copies are never sent.

Any device

iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux. The receiving side is just a browser tab.

Commands

Everything else.

toss send Send your clipboard without the hotkey
toss send notes.pdf Send a file by name
toss status Your code, address and settings
toss serve --new-code Rotate the code, cut old devices off
toss serve --key capslock Hold a different key
toss uninstall Stop starting on login

Before you try it

College wifi will block it

Client isolation stops devices talking directly. Home wifi works. A phone hotspot works.

The hotkey is Windows only

Elsewhere everything works except hold-to-send. Use toss send.

iPhone needs one tap

Safari blocks websites from writing your clipboard on their own. Copies arrive pinned, one tap to copy.
