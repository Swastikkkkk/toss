    One Shortcut turns toss into a share option, like AirDrop. Share anything, tap toss, it's on your laptop. Double-tap the back of your phone and the laptop's clipboard is yours.

    1. In Shortcuts tap +, name it toss. Tap ⓘ and turn on Show in Share Sheet.
    2. Add If: Shortcut Input has any value.
      Inside it: Get Contents of URL, method POST, body File = Shortcut Input.
      Under Otherwise: Get Contents of URL, then Copy to Clipboard.
      Both use this link:
      open toss on your wifi to get your link
    3. Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap › Double Tap › toss.
